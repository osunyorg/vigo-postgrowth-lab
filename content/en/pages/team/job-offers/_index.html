FAIR-CYCLE PROJECT – POSTDOCTORAL RESEARCHER (START DATE: JANUARY 2027)

We are
seeking to recruit a junior postdoctoral researcher to join the FAIR-CYCLE project from 1 January 2027. The successful
candidate will hold a PhD in management, ecology, ecological economics or a
related field . The contract will run for four years, with the possibility
of extension after the project has ended, provided that new projects are
secured or specific funding for research staff is obtained.


Place of
work: Pontevedra
(Spain)


Workday : Full Time (37.5 hours per week)


Gross
monthly salary : 2.300,00
€ (including the apportionments of extra payments)

Deadline for applications: 

23:59 on 18 November 2026

ACHIEVEMENTS
TO BE CONSIDERED


Academic
background. Experience in qualitative research. Proficiency in English and
Spanish (30 points)


Education
and professional experience in research in fields related to the circular
economy, just transition, ecological economics, innovation for sustainability,
and public management (20 points)


Publications
in the fields of just transition and the circular economy. Participation in
European projects (20 points)


Professional
experience in the private sector or civil service related to the green
transition (15 points)


Personal
interview (15 points). The interview will be conducted to a maximum of 3
candidates who, in the sum of the merits, reach a minimum of 50 points

HOW TO
APPLY

Applications
must be submitted electronically via the university's website only ( https://sede.uvigo.gal/ ), by going to ' Procedementos
e trámites/PAS/PDI ' and clicking on the link 'SRHI - 1426 - Concurso de méritos
para a contratación de persoal de investigación'. Please state ONLY THE
REFERENCE NUMBER OF THE VACANCY in the subject line when submitting. As an
exception to this procedure, and solely for non-EU foreign nationals,
alternative electronic means of identification may be used . To do so,
applicants must initiate the process by sending an email to
estranxeiros@uvigo.gal at least two working days before the deadline for
submitting applications.

The deadline
for submitting applications is 23:59 on 18 November 2026 (ten working days
from the day following publication of this call on the University of Vigo's
electronic noticeboard).

The
following documentation must be submitted alongside the application:


An Abbreviated
Curriculum Vitae CVA (AEI) in accordance with the standard template
available at https://cvn.fecyt.es/ . Should you wish to expand on the
content, a single additional sheet containing further information may be
attached. Only one file must be submitted.


A sworn
statement (Annex II) confirming compliance with the requirements set out in
the terms and conditions of the call for applications and the accuracy of the
information provided in the CVA.

At the
request of the selection committees, the Research Human Resources Service may
ask candidates to provide the original documents proving the requirements and
merits declared.

Please
carefully download and read the information contained in the merit-based
competition before submitting your application: https://sede.uvigo.gal/public/bulletin/bulletin-index.xhtml

We are seeking to recruit a junior postdoctoral researcher to join the FAIR-CYCLE project from 1 January 2027. The successful candidate will hold a PhD in management, ecology, ecological economics or a related field. The contract will run for four years, with the possibility of extension after the project has ended, provided that new projects are secured or specific funding for research staff is obtained.

  • Place of work: Pontevedra (Spain)
  • Workday: Full Time (37.5 hours per week)
  • Gross monthly salary: 2.300,00 € (including the apportionments of extra payments)
  • Deadline for applications:  23:59 on 18 November 2026

ACHIEVEMENTS TO BE CONSIDERED

  1. Academic background. Experience in qualitative research. Proficiency in English and Spanish (30 points)
  2. Education and professional experience in research in fields related to the circular economy, just transition, ecological economics, innovation for sustainability, and public management (20 points)
  3. Publications in the fields of just transition and the circular economy. Participation in European projects (20 points)
  4. Professional experience in the private sector or civil service related to the green transition (15 points)
  5. Personal interview (15 points). The interview will be conducted to a maximum of 3 candidates who, in the sum of the merits, reach a minimum of 50 points

HOW TO APPLY

Applications must be submitted electronically via the university's website only (https://sede.uvigo.gal/), by going to 'Procedementos e trámites/PAS/PDI' and clicking on the link 'SRHI - 1426 - Concurso de méritos para a contratación de persoal de investigación'. Please state ONLY THE REFERENCE NUMBER OF THE VACANCY in the subject line when submitting. As an exception to this procedure, and solely for non-EU foreign nationals, alternative electronic means of identification may be used. To do so, applicants must initiate the process by sending an email to estranxeiros@uvigo.gal at least two working days before the deadline for submitting applications.

The deadline for submitting applications is 23:59 on 18 November 2026 (ten working days from the day following publication of this call on the University of Vigo's electronic noticeboard).

The following documentation must be submitted alongside the application:

  • An Abbreviated Curriculum Vitae CVA (AEI) in accordance with the standard template available at https://cvn.fecyt.es/. Should you wish to expand on the content, a single additional sheet containing further information may be attached. Only one file must be submitted.
  • A sworn statement (Annex II) confirming compliance with the requirements set out in the terms and conditions of the call for applications and the accuracy of the information provided in the CVA.

At the request of the selection committees, the Research Human Resources Service may ask candidates to provide the original documents proving the requirements and merits declared. Please carefully download and read the information contained in the merit-based competition before submitting your application: https://sede.uvigo.gal/public/bulletin/bulletin-index.xhtml
