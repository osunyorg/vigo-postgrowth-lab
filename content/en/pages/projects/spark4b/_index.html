From alternative ideas to practical pathways

SPARK4B+ starts by mapping alternative paradigms, and builds the tools to test them: participatory visioning methods linked with biodiversity, climate and economic models so trade-offs become visible. Those tools are then applied in six places, across seven sectors: agriculture, forestry, water, finance, health, education and urban development. And in Budapest, Hungary, under-used parks and cemeteries reimagined as spaces for mental and physical restoration.

Each site runs in two stages: a Transformation Lab where stakeholders imagine a biodiversity-positive and just future, then a Demonstration Intervention where the best idea is trialled and measured - for biodiversity, climate resilience, social equity and economic value.

But a pilot that ends with the project changes nothing. So, SPARK4B+ also builds coalitions meant to outlast the project, champions who carry practices into their own institutions, and communication that reaches past the usual audiences.

A multidisciplinary international consortium

SPARK4B+ is coordinated by Coventry University through its Centre for Agroecology, Water and Resilience (CAWR) and co-coordinated by GreenFormation (Hungary). The wider consortium brings together universities, research institutes, SMEs, civil-society organisations and policy actors from across Europe and Colombia. The project is further supported by an Advisory and Influencers Board, connecting perspectives from Europe, Latin America, Africa and beyond.The SPARK4B+ project website is live at spark4bplus.eu, LinkedIn and BlueSky.
