Our director, Mario Pansera, delivered the lecture  Science, technology and innovation in a post-growth world  at UCAS, as part of the Zhongguancun Lecture Series in Social Sciences (Z-LiSS), on 20 October at the Zhongguancun Campus in Beijing. The session was organised by the Sino-Danish College (SDC) and hosted by Professor Sun Qian. Mario is currently in China as a visiting scholar at UCAS under the PIFI programme, and in the coming weeks he will also teach on the UCAS-CBS Master's in Public Management and Social Development.

Our director, Mario Pansera, delivered the lecture Science, technology and innovation in a post-growth world at UCAS, as part of the Zhongguancun Lecture Series in Social Sciences (Z-LiSS), on 20 October at the Zhongguancun Campus in Beijing. The session was organised by the Sino-Danish College (SDC) and hosted by Professor Sun Qian.

Mario is currently in China as a visiting scholar at UCAS under the PIFI programme, and in the coming weeks he will also teach on the UCAS-CBS Master's in Public Management and Social Development.
