Ana has a double BSc in Advertising and Public Relations with Marketing from Rey Juan Carlos University and an MSc in Economic Development and Innovation, specialising in Sustainable Development and Circular Economy from the University of Santiago de Compostela.

At the lab, Ana's PhD explores the intersection of culture, food consumption and the ecological transition. She examines the notion of healthy and sustainable diets to understand how different actors (institutions, citizen organisations, doctors, etc.) define what it means to be a responsible consumer and equip consumers to make responsible decisions, focusing on how health and sustainability criteria are negotiated in practice.

Ana has experience working in marketing, hospitality, and retail. She enjoys spending her free time reading, indulging in creative activities, and taking long walks in nature.
